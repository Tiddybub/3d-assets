## FAQ

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. You edit a spreadsheet and run one command. I set everything up and include written instructions, and the Premium package adds a screen-recorded walkthrough.

**Can it match my existing invoice design?**
Yes. Send me a PDF of an invoice you already send and I'll rebuild that layout, including your logo, colours and payment details.

**Does it produce PDFs?**
It produces a print-ready document that saves to PDF from any browser in two clicks. If you need direct PDF files with no browser step, mention it before ordering and I'll include it.

**Can it email invoices to clients?**
Yes, as a gig extra. It sends from your own email account.

**Will it handle VAT / GST / sales tax?**
Yes — a default rate plus per-client and per-line overrides, so exempt and zero-rated clients are handled properly.

**Can it do recurring or retainer invoices?**
Yes, that's part of the Premium package. Tell me your billing cycle and I'll set it up.

**What if my numbers need to match my old invoice sequence?**
Tell me your last invoice number and format, and the tool continues from there.

## Buyer requirements

1. Your business details as they should appear: name, address, email, phone, tax/VAT ID.
2. Your logo (PNG or SVG), and brand colours if you have them.
3. A PDF or photo of an invoice you currently send, if you want the design matched.
4. Payment terms (Net 14, Net 30…) and payment details to print on the invoice.
5. Currency, tax rate(s), and whether any clients are tax-exempt.
6. A sample of your line items — a spreadsheet, or just a few example rows.
