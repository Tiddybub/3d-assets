## FAQ

**Will my original photos be changed?**
Never. Everything is written to a new folder and the originals are left exactly as they were.

**What sizes should I use for my website?**
If you're not sure, tell me your platform (Shopify, WooCommerce, Squarespace, Wix…) and I'll use the sizes it expects.

**Can you watermark with my logo instead of text?**
Yes — send a PNG with a transparent background and tell me the position and how strong you want it.

**Will the images lose quality?**
Compression is tuned so the difference isn't visible at normal viewing size. If you want a specific file-size target instead, tell me the limit and I'll hit it.

**Do you keep the folder structure?**
Yes by default, or I can flatten everything into one folder — your choice.

**Can I send a Google Drive or Dropbox link?**
Yes, that's usually easier than uploading. WeTransfer works too.

**Does it strip EXIF / location data?**
Yes, by default — useful for privacy. Say the word if you'd rather keep it, e.g. for photography archives.

**Can it handle 50,000 images?**
Yes. Message me first so we can agree a delivery window and a transfer method.
