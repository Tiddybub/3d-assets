## FAQ

**Can you scrape a site that needs a login?**
Only if it's an account you own or are authorised to use, and the site's terms allow it. I don't bypass logins, paywalls or captchas. Ask me first and I'll tell you honestly whether it's possible.

**Is web scraping legal?**
Collecting publicly visible information generally is, but it depends on the site's terms of service and on what the data is. I don't collect personal data without a lawful basis, and I'll flag anything I think is a problem before you order rather than after.

**What if the website changes?**
Layout changes are the usual reason a scraper stops working. Premium buyers get the config file and instructions to update selectors themselves; I also offer a monthly refresh as an extra.

**How many rows can you get?**
Basic covers 500 and Standard 10,000. Beyond that, message me — large jobs are fine, they just need a delivery window that keeps the crawl polite.

**Can you deliver straight to Google Sheets?**
Yes, as a gig extra.

**Can you scrape Amazon / LinkedIn / Facebook?**
These actively block scraping and their terms prohibit it. I don't take those jobs. Ask me about the specific site and I'll tell you before you order.

**What do you need from me to start?**
The URL, and a screenshot or list of the fields you want as columns.
